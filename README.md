# Kwiklance

> *build your brand. grow your business.*


<img width="433" alt="Kwiklance Logo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d7b5aabc-088d-4695-af75-60f3da8fa237" />

A web-based freelance marketplace platform where clients can explore services, commission projects, and connect with skilled professionals across multiple creative and technical industries.

---

> <font color="red">**Note:** This project is currently unfinished and under active development. Features and pages are subject to change.

---

## Live Demo

You can view and test the live application directly at:  
**[https://kwiklance.vercel.app/](https://kwiklance.vercel.app/)**

---

## Tech Stack

* **HTML5** – Semantic layouts and modular components
* **CSS3** – Custom responsive styles and grid layouts
* **JavaScript (ES6+)** – Interactive scripts and component loaders (`include.js`)
* **FontAwesome 6** – Interface icons

---

## Key Pages

* **Authentication:** `login.html`, `register.html`, `forgot.html`, `reset.html`
* **Home & Directory:** `home.html`, `navigation.html`, `footer.html`
* **Explore Categories:**
  * `digital.html` – Digital Marketing & Media
  * `graphic.html` – Graphics & Design
  * `music.html` – Music & Audio
  * `personal.html` – Personal Growth & Hobbies
  * `photo.html` – Photography & Editing
  * `program.html` – Programming & Tech
  * `video.html` – Video & Animation
  * `write.html` – Writing & Translation
